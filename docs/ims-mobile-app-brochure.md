## Front Cover

# IMS Mobile

## National reporting. One pipeline. Clear accountability.

IMS Mobile helps ministry leaders submit structured reports from the field, receive feedback, and stay connected to the national review process. From unit activity to state ministry reports, the app brings reporting into one trusted system that leadership can review with confidence.

**Suggested cover line:**  
Built for disciplined ministry reporting, national visibility, and executive follow-through.

## Inside Cover: Why IMS Mobile Exists

Ministry work produces valuable information every week: attendance, offerings, testimonies, programme updates, operational challenges, and strategic observations. When this information is scattered across messages, papers, and separate files, leadership loses speed and clarity.

IMS Mobile solves this by giving approved users a simple way to submit the right report, at the right time, into the right review chain.

The app is designed for:

- Field-level reporting.
- Directorate accountability.
- State ministry coordination.
- Executive administration.
- Faster feedback and clearer decisions.

## The App Experience

IMS Mobile is organised around five main areas: Home, Inbox, Submit, History, and Profile. Each area has a clear purpose, so users do not need technical training to understand the reporting journey.

The experience is intentionally simple: sign in, understand your role, submit the right report, receive feedback, and keep your own record.

## 1. Login and Account Creation

**Purpose:** secure access for approved users.

The login screen allows returning users to sign in with their email and password. New users can create an account using role-aware registration.

During account creation, users are guided according to their responsibility:

- Directorate users select their directorate and unit where applicable.
- State Coordinators select their assigned state or the Federal Capital Territory.
- The system stores each user profile securely in the IMS backend.

**Leadership value:** every report is connected to a known person, role, and organisational assignment.

**Screenshot suggestion:** show the login screen and the create account flow with state selection.

## 2. Home

**Purpose:** personal dashboard and reporting guidance.

The Home screen welcomes the user and shows their current role. It displays either the user's directorate context or, for State Coordinators, their state context.

The Home screen helps the user understand:

- Their role in the reporting structure.
- Their key responsibilities.
- The kind of report expected from them.
- The reporting focus for their level of ministry work.

For State Coordinators, the Home screen presents the state ministry reporting focus and reminds them that their reports go directly to national administration.

**Leadership value:** users are not just given a blank form; they are reminded of their duty and guided toward useful reporting.

**Screenshot suggestion:** show a State Coordinator home screen and a directorate user home screen side by side.

## 3. Submit

**Purpose:** structured report creation.

The Submit screen is where users create their reports. The app presents fields based on the user's role and organisational context.

Directorate users can submit reports aligned with their directorate framework. State Coordinators submit state ministry reports with fields such as:

- Total attendance.
- Total offering.
- Testimonies and ministry updates.

The report form is designed to capture both measurable data and meaningful narrative. This helps leadership see numbers and also understand what is happening spiritually, administratively, and operationally.

**Leadership value:** reports become easier to review because the system captures consistent information in a structured format.

**Screenshot suggestion:** show the Submit screen with the report form and visible metric fields.

## 4. Inbox

**Purpose:** receive decisions, feedback, and action notices.

The Inbox screen helps users stay informed about what has happened to their reports. Instead of waiting for informal follow-up, users can see messages connected to report decisions and review actions.

The Inbox can support notices such as:

- Report approved.
- Report rejected or declined.
- Revision requested.
- Report forwarded to another review stage.

When a revision is required, the user can open the related report and move into the resubmission flow.

**Leadership value:** feedback becomes official, traceable, and easier to act on.

**Screenshot suggestion:** show inbox notices with a revision request or approval message.

## 5. Review and Resubmit

**Purpose:** correction without losing accountability.

When a report requires changes, the app supports a guided resubmission path. This means corrections are not handled outside the system through scattered messages.

The user can review what needs attention, make the necessary updates, and submit the revised report back into the proper workflow.

**Leadership value:** revisions remain connected to the original report record, preserving the history of what happened and why.

**Screenshot suggestion:** show the report review screen and the resubmit screen.

## 6. History

**Purpose:** personal record of submitted reports.

The History screen shows the user's past reports and their statuses. This creates confidence that work has been submitted and allows users to track their own reporting discipline.

Users can refer back to previous submissions when preparing for meetings, follow-up, or future reporting cycles.

**Leadership value:** authors have their own transparent record and do not need to depend on memory or private messages.

**Screenshot suggestion:** show a list of submitted reports with visible status indicators.

## 7. Profile

**Purpose:** identity and assignment clarity.

The Profile screen shows the user's account details, role, and organisational placement.

For directorate users, the profile shows their directorate or unit context. For State Coordinators, it shows their assigned state.

**Leadership value:** every user operates from a clear identity and reporting assignment.

**Screenshot suggestion:** show a profile screen for a State Coordinator, highlighting the state field.

## Who Benefits

### Unit Heads

Unit Heads gain a simple way to submit operational reports from their area of responsibility. Their reports become visible to the appropriate review chain and remain part of the organisation's record.

### Managers

Managers benefit from clearer reporting discipline and can participate in review or forwarding processes where applicable. This reduces uncertainty and keeps reports moving.

### Directors

Directors gain stronger visibility into directorate-level work and can submit strategic reports that preserve both metrics and narrative.

### State Coordinators

State Coordinators now have a national reporting channel. Their reports are not treated as side records or local forms. They are part of the same IMS pipeline and are routed directly to national administration.

### National Leadership

The National Director of Administration (NDA), Associate General Overseer (AGO), and General Overseer (GO) gain clearer visibility, more consistent reports, and a traceable path from submission to decision.

## What Makes IMS Mobile Different

IMS Mobile is not just a form app. It is connected to a full administrative system.

It provides:

- Secure sign-in.
- Role-based reporting.
- State Coordinator onboarding for all 37 Nigerian jurisdictions.
- Structured metrics and narrative reports.
- Inbox feedback and revision handling.
- Submission history.
- Connection to the national executive review workflow.
